# Code of Conduct

STEJECT is built for students, many of whom are making their first open-source contribution. We want this to be a place where anyone can learn, ask "obvious" questions and make mistakes without being made to feel small.

## Our standards

Do:
- Be welcoming and patient, especially with beginners.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Explain the *why* when you ask for a change.
- Credit other people's ideas and code.

Don't:
- Harass, insult, or make demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, skill level or English.
- Share someone else's private information without permission.
- Post sexual content, threats, or anything meant to intimidate.
- Keep arguing after a maintainer has asked you to stop.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, commits) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through the email on [@aadvik7's GitHub profile](https://github.com/aadvik7), or use GitHub's *Report content* option. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens next

Maintainers will review every report and may ask the person to change their behavior, remove their comments or commits, or block them from the project, depending on how serious and repeated it is.

This code is adapted from the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
